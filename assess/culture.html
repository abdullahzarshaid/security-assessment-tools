What this assessment covers

The 12 prompts, grouped by area. Each one has a "what to look for" note and a concrete action inside the assessment.

Roles
  • Do people know the security decisions expected in their role?
  • Can managers explain who accepts risk and exceptions?
  • Are security responsibilities included when roles change?
Learning
  • Does training use scenarios people actually encounter?
  • Can participants explain why an answer is safer?
  • Are accessibility, language and time constraints addressed?
Practice
  • Do teams rehearse ambiguous situations without harming real systems?
  • Are technical and executive decisions exercised together?
  • Does each exercise end with owned improvements?
Feedback
  • Can people report mistakes and suspicious activity without fear or delay?
  • Are training needs informed by incidents and control changes?
  • Is programme success measured beyond attendance and clicks?
How scoring works. Only rated prompts count toward the index. Not sure and unanswered prompts are reported separately and never scored as a pass or a gap. Gaps are listed highest priority first, each with a link to a matching template in the open GRC Documentation Kit. This is a conversation starter, not an audit or a certification.
