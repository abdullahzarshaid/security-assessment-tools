Scope. 12 original prompts for AI-enabled applications. No model testing, certification, exhaustive OWASP mapping or legal applicability determination. Reference: NIST AI Risk Management Framework (context only, not an endorsement).

What this assessment covers

The 12 prompts, grouped by area. Each one has a "what to look for" note and a concrete action inside the assessment.

Purpose
  • Is each AI use case inventoried with an accountable owner?
  • Are high-impact uses identified before deployment?
  • Are acceptable uses and prohibited inputs documented?
Data
  • Are sensitive inputs limited to approved systems and purposes?
  • Does retrieval enforce the requesting user’s access?
  • Can you remove or correct retained AI data where required?
Authority
  • Are tool actions authorized outside the model?
  • Do consequential actions require specific, reviewable approval?
  • Can agent credentials be revoked quickly?
Evaluation
  • Have untrusted-content and instruction-injection cases been tested?
  • Are failures logged without unnecessarily retaining sensitive content?
  • Is there a release decision and monitoring owner?
How scoring works. Only rated prompts count toward the index. Not sure and unanswered prompts are reported separately and never scored as a pass or a gap. Gaps are listed highest priority first, each with a link to a matching template in the open GRC Documentation Kit. This is a conversation starter, not an audit or a certification.
