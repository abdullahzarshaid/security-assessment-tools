What this assessment covers

The 12 prompts, grouped by area. Each one has a "what to look for" note and a concrete action inside the assessment.

Signals
  • Are priority threats linked to the logs you collect?
  • Can you detect missing or stale log sources?
  • Are alerting rules tested against expected signals?
Triage
  • Does every alert queue have an accountable reviewer?
  • Are severity and escalation criteria understood?
  • Can analysts retrieve asset and identity context?
Response
  • Are containment decisions authorized and documented?
  • Are investigation records preserved with appropriate access?
  • Are communication responsibilities and external obligations reviewed?
Learning
  • Has a realistic tabletop exposed decision gaps?
  • Are post-incident improvements assigned and tracked?
  • Do service owners participate in recovery validation?
How scoring works. Only rated prompts count toward the index. Not sure and unanswered prompts are reported separately and never scored as a pass or a gap. Gaps are listed highest priority first, each with a link to a matching template in the open GRC Documentation Kit. This is a conversation starter, not an audit or a certification.
