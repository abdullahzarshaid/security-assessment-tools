What this assessment covers

The 12 prompts, grouped by area. Each one has a "what to look for" note and a concrete action inside the assessment.

Inventory
  • Are human, service and emergency identities owned?
  • Can important permissions be explained in business terms?
  • Are unmanaged or shared accounts identified?
Authentication
  • Is phishing-resistant authentication prioritized for high-impact access?
  • Are account-recovery paths at least as well protected as sign-in?
  • Are session lifetime and reauthentication decisions explicit?
Privilege
  • Is privileged access separate from routine work?
  • Are temporary privileges time-bounded and reviewed?
  • Can consequential service identities act beyond their purpose?
Lifecycle
  • Are joiner, mover and leaver changes linked to authoritative events?
  • Does revocation end active authority as intended?
  • Are access reviews focused on meaningful decisions?
How scoring works. Only rated prompts count toward the index. Not sure and unanswered prompts are reported separately and never scored as a pass or a gap.
