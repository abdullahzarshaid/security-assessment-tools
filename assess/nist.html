What this assessment covers

The 18 prompts, grouped by area. Each one has a "what to look for" note and a concrete action inside the assessment.

Govern
  • Does security have a named decision-maker?
  • Are important risks reviewed with business leaders?
  • Are critical suppliers included in your security decisions?
Identify
  • Do you know which systems and services matter most?
  • Do you understand where sensitive information flows?
  • Are vulnerabilities tracked through remediation?
Protect
  • Is stronger authentication enforced on important accounts?
  • Is access removed or changed when roles change?
  • Are baseline configurations and updates maintained?
Detect
  • Are security-relevant logs available for important services?
  • Does someone review and act on security alerts?
  • Have detection gaps been tested recently?
Respond
  • Can the team find an incident contact and decision process?
  • Are containment and evidence-preservation decisions planned?
  • Has an incident scenario been rehearsed?
Recover
  • Have important backups been restored in a test?
  • Are recovery priorities agreed with service owners?
  • Do recovery exercises feed back into the security plan?
How scoring works. Only rated prompts count toward the index. Not sure and unanswered prompts are reported separately and never scored as a pass or a gap. Gaps are listed highest priority first, each with a link to a matching template in the open GRC Documentation Kit. This is a conversation starter, not an audit or a certification.
