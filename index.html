How scoring works, honestly. Each prompt is rated as Operating, Partial, Planned, Not in place, or Not sure. The index is the sum of your rated states divided by the number of rated prompts times three. Prompts you mark Not sure, and prompts you leave unanswered, are shown separately and are never counted as a pass or as a gap. The result is a conversation starter for your own team, not an audit, a certification, or a compliance determination, and scores across different assessments are not comparable.
Private by design. There is no account, no server and no tracking. Your answers are stored only in this browser so you can come back to them, and you can clear them at any time with the Reset button inside an assessment.
